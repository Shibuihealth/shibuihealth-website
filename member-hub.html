What The Circle is

The Circle is a private online space for education and connection during pregnancy and the early months with a baby. It is one of the optional services offered within Reborn, our midwifery-led antenatal and postnatal care.

You sign in to the community with your own member login, and it opens at community.shibuihealth.com.au.

Good to know

A few things to keep in mind before you join.

Led by Jenna Keygan, registered nurse and endorsed midwife. More about Jenna.
General information Content in The Circle is general education. It isn't individual medical advice and doesn't replace care from your own midwife, doctor or nurse practitioner.
Your privacy Other members can see what you post, so please don't share personal health or clinical details in the community. Our privacy notice explains how we handle member information.
Urgent concerns The Circle isn't an urgent-care channel. If you're worried about your health or your baby's, contact your care provider, or call 000 in an emergency.
Questions Not sure if The Circle is right for you? Send us an enquiry and we'll be in touch.
