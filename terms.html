Terms

The terms that apply when you book, join or enrol.

Shibui Health Pty Ltd provides nurse practitioner consultations, midwifery-led care, The Circle membership and HypnoBirthing® childbirth education. Each has its own cancellation and refund terms, set out or linked below. Last updated 23 November 2026.

Not for emergencies. If you or someone else has a life-threatening condition or is in danger, call 000 now. Shibui Health does not provide emergency care.

Consultations

Consultation fees are published on our Care & Pricing page and are the same for everyone. Fees are payable at the time of the consultation. Online consultations are a private fee; a Medicare rebate may apply depending on your eligibility. Fees cover the consultation only — any tests, treatments or products are costed separately and quoted before they are arranged.

Some problems can't be safely assessed online. If that applies to you, we'll tell you during the consultation and advise on next steps. A consultation that can't proceed for clinical reasons is not charged.

If you need to change or cancel an appointment, please give as much notice as you can so the time can be offered to someone else.

The Circle — monthly membership

The monthly membership is a subscription, billed each month until you cancel. You can cancel at any time from your billing area, with no lock-in and no cancellation fee. Cancelling stops future payments; the month you have already paid for runs to its end. There is no joining fee.

The Circle — 12-month memberships

The 12-month memberships are a single payment rather than a subscription. Nothing renews automatically and nothing is charged again. Because they are priced at ten months' fees, they carry their own cancellation and refund terms, including a 7-day change-of-mind period.

Read the 12-month membership cancellation and refund terms →

HypnoBirthing® courses

Courses are five weekly sessions of 2.5 hours, enrolled as one package. Sessions build on each other and are not sold individually. Your place is held once payment is received and your signed enrolment agreement is returned. Courses have their own tiered cancellation and refund terms, with a full refund available if your circumstances change for medical reasons or because of pregnancy loss, whatever notice you are able to give.

Read the HypnoBirthing® cancellation and refund terms →

HypnoBirthing® is a standalone childbirth education course. It is never required in order to receive midwifery care, and midwifery care is never required in order to attend.

Payments

How we take payment. Online payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card details. Your receipt comes from Stripe, and for subscriptions you can update your card, view receipts or cancel from your Stripe billing area.

GST. Where GST applies, the price shown is inclusive of it.

Buy now, pay later. Where Afterpay or Zip is offered, your payment agreement is with that provider, not with Shibui Health. Any refund is processed back through them and your remaining instalments are adjusted accordingly. You remain responsible for instalments until the refund is processed. Their own terms, fees and eligibility rules apply.

Currency. All prices are in Australian dollars.

Clinical care and scope

Our clinicians work within their registration, scope of practice and professional obligations. Nothing on this website is personal medical advice, and the educational content in The Circle and in HypnoBirthing® classes is general information and community support — it does not replace individual care from your own midwife, GP or obstetric team.

Jenna does not attend homebirths, and midwifery care through Reborn does not include attendance at your birth.

Your rights

Nothing in these terms limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law, including the consumer guarantees that apply to services. Where a term in these pages conflicts with those rights, your rights prevail.

Privacy

How we collect, use and protect your information is set out in our privacy notice.
