Questions, answered

The short version of how The Circle works.

When does The Circle open?

In 2027. Jen is still building it, and she'd rather open it properly than early. Register your interest below and you'll hear as soon as there's a date — everything on this page is what it will include when it opens.

Do I need to be pregnant to join?

No. Members include women planning a pregnancy, pregnant women and new mothers.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, on the monthly membership. Cancel from your billing area, with no lock-in. The 12-month membership is paid upfront and covers a set twelve months.

Can I pay for a year upfront?

Yes. The Circle is $590 for 12 months, and The Circle with Village is $880 — each the price of ten months, so two are free. You can pay by card, Afterpay or Zip. Both are a single payment rather than a subscription, so nothing renews automatically — Jen will be in touch before your twelve months are up to ask whether you'd like to continue. See the 12-month membership terms for how cancellations and refunds work.

Are sessions recorded?

The monthly education session is recorded for members. The Ask Jen call and mothers' group are not.

Is this medical advice?

No. The Circle is education and community support. See the note above.

How do I book one-to-one care with Jen?

Members get priority booking for Jen's antenatal and postnatal care packages. Send an enquiry and mention that you're a Circle member.

When does Village start?

It's available for the first 12 months postpartum, and you can add it when you're ready.

12-month membership — cancellation & refunds

What happens if your plans change, or ours do. These terms apply to the 12-month memberships only, from the day they go on sale in 2027; the monthly membership can be cancelled anytime from your billing area.

Changing your mind in the first 7 days. If you cancel within 7 days of joining, you'll receive a full refund, whether or not you've attended a session.

Cancelling after that. The 12-month membership is priced at ten months' fees, so the two free months are a benefit of completing the full term. If you cancel partway through, we'll refund what you've paid, less the standard monthly rate for each complete month you've had access.

For example: The Circle at $590, cancelled four months in — four months at $59 is $236, so your refund is $354. The Circle with Village at $880, cancelled four months in — four months at $88 is $352, so your refund is $528. If the value you've used is more than you paid, no refund is due, but you'll never be asked for more.

If we can't deliver. If The Circle stops running, or we can't provide what you've paid for, you'll receive a full refund of the remaining portion with no monthly-rate deduction.

Village eligibility. Village is designed for the first 12 months postpartum. If you pass that point during your membership, your access continues to the end of the twelve months you've paid for.

No automatic renewal. The 12-month membership is a single payment, not a subscription. Nothing renews and nothing is charged again. Jen will contact you before your term ends to ask whether you'd like to continue.

Paying by instalments. If you pay using Afterpay, Zip or another buy-now-pay-later option, your payment agreement is with that provider, not with Shibui Health. Any refund is processed back through them and your remaining instalments are adjusted accordingly. You remain responsible for instalments until the refund is processed.

Your rights. Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

How to cancel. Email admin@shibuihealth.com.au. The date we receive your message is the date used above, and refunds are processed within 10 business days.
