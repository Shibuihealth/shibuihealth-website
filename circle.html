Questions, answered

The short version of how The Circle works.

Do I need to be pregnant to join?

No. Members include women planning a pregnancy, pregnant women and new mothers.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel from your billing area, with no lock-in.

Are sessions recorded?

The monthly education session is recorded for members. The Ask Jen call and mothers' group are not.

Is this medical advice?

No. See the disclaimer below.

How do I book one-to-one care with Jen?

Members get priority booking and 10% off à la carte services.
