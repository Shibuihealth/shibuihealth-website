About

Independent, small, and built around who actually sees you.

Shibui Health is a nurse practitioner and midwifery-led practice based in Wynyard, on Tasmania's North-West coast — started to bring unhurried, individualised care to a region that's often had to travel for it.

渋いshibui
Shibui describes a beauty that doesn't announce itself — plain at first glance, considered underneath.

We borrowed the word because it describes the kind of practice we wanted to run: clinicians with the time to take a proper history, think it through, and build a plan around the person in front of them — quietly rigorous, never performative.

The team

Two clinicians. You'll get to know both of them.

AK

Andrew Keygan

Director & Nurse Practitioner

Andrew is a dual master's-qualified Nurse Practitioner with 18 years' experience across emergency and primary care. Alongside his clinical work, his career rests on a strong foundation in quality, clinical governance and compliance — the systems that make good care safe, consistent and accountable, not just good on the day.

He has led multidisciplinary teams and organisations at both local and national level. As founder and director of Shibui Health, Andrew sees patients across the Repair and Reshape clinics.

JK

Jenna Keygan

Director, Registered Nurse & Endorsed Midwife

Jenna is a Registered Nurse and Endorsed Midwife with experience in neonatal care — so her focus doesn't stop at birth, it carries through to your baby's first weeks.

An experienced workforce leader and a committed patient advocate, she leads Reborn, Shibui Health's midwifery-led antenatal and postnatal care, with one known midwife the whole way through rather than whoever's on shift.

Jenna is also a certified HypnoBirthing® Childbirth Educator and teaches HypnoBirthing® classes locally. The classes are a separate childbirth education course, offered independently of her midwifery care. More about HypnoBirthing® →
