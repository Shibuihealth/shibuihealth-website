Birth doesn't have to be something that happens to you.

HypnoBirthing® is a childbirth education program built around the idea that good preparation can help you feel calm, informed and involved in your birth, whatever it ends up looking like. It teaches breathing, deep relaxation and visualisation techniques, with the aim of a calmer, more comfortable birth experience. It doesn't promise a painless birth, and it doesn't replace advice from your own care team.

What the classes cover

Practical techniques, not just theory — for you and your birth partner.

Breathing & relaxation Breathing and deep-relaxation techniques to help you stay calm and cope with the intensity of labour.
Visualisation Guided visualisation practice to help you work with your body through labour rather than tensing against it.
Informed choice Skills for asking questions and weighing your options, so you can take part in decisions about your care.
Your birth partner Classes are built for two — your partner learns the techniques alongside you and how to support you through labour.
Any birth plan The techniques are taught to support a calm, informed experience whether you're planning a vaginal birth or a caesarean, in hospital or at home. These are education classes; they don't include attendance at your birth.

Good to know

The practical details.

Format Five weekly sessions of 2.5 hours, taught in person.
The method HypnoBirthing® International (The Mongan Method). Jenna is certified through the HypnoBirthing® Institute.
Dates & pricing Course fees are separate from consultation fees. Class dates and fees are confirmed directly with Jenna — send an enquiry to find out when the next course starts.
Separate from midwifery care The course is enrolled in, priced and recorded separately from Reborn. You can take the classes without being a midwifery client, and midwifery care never requires them.
Who it's for Open to anyone expecting, regardless of where you're planning to give birth or who's providing your clinical care.
Your own care team The classes teach techniques and informed decision-making. For anything clinical, ask your own midwife, doctor or hospital team.
