Birth doesn't have to be something that happens to you.

HypnoBirthing® is a childbirth education program built around the idea that good preparation can help you feel calm, informed and involved in your birth, whatever it ends up looking like. It teaches breathing, deep relaxation and visualisation techniques, with the aim of a calmer, more comfortable birth experience. While the course doesn't promise a painless birth, it teaches tools and techniques designed to help you work with your body's own natural endorphins.

What the classes cover

Practical techniques, not just theory — for you and your birth partner.

Breathing & relaxation Breathing and deep-relaxation techniques to help you stay calm and cope with the intensity of labour.
Visualisation Guided visualisation practice to help you work with your body through labour rather than tensing against it.
Informed choice Skills for asking questions and weighing your options, so you can take part in decisions about your care.
Your birth partner Classes are built for two — your partner learns the techniques alongside you and how to support you through labour.

What a course looks like

Five weekly sessions that build on each other, taught in person.

In the room Taught in person by Jenna, with time to practise the techniques together and to ask questions.

Common questions

The things people ask before they enrol.

When should I start?

Send us your due date and we'll tell you which upcoming course suits your timing. You don't need to be sure yet — registering your interest doesn't commit you to anything.

Does my partner need to come?

Classes are built for two, and your partner or chosen support person is warmly encouraged to attend so you can practise the techniques together. If you're coming on your own, get in touch and we'll talk it through.

Does it replace my midwife or doctor?

No. The classes teach techniques and informed decision-making. For anything clinical, ask your own midwife, doctor or hospital team.

What if my birth doesn't go to plan?

The techniques are taught to support a calm, informed experience whatever happens, including an induction, a caesarean or a change of plan. The course can't promise how your birth will go.

Will Jenna be at my birth?

No. These are education classes and don't include attendance at your birth.

How much does it cost, and how do I enrol?

Course fees are separate from consultation fees. Send an enquiry and we'll reply with upcoming dates, fees and how to enrol.

Cancellation & refunds

What happens if your plans change, or ours do.

What you're enrolling in. The course is five weekly sessions of 2.5 hours, enrolled as one package. The sessions build on each other and are not sold individually.

Confirming your place. Your place is held once payment is received and your signed enrolment agreement is returned. Places are limited to four couples and are confirmed in the order they are completed.

If you need to cancel

  • 14 days or more before the first session — full refund, less the cost of any materials already ordered for you. Or transfer your place to the next intake at no charge.
  • 7 to 13 days before — 50% refund, or transfer your full payment to the next intake.
  • Less than 7 days before, or after the course has started — no refund. Jenna will offer a transfer to the next intake where she can, but it isn't guaranteed.

If your circumstances change. If your baby arrives early, if you experience pregnancy loss, or if you're advised on medical grounds not to attend, you'll receive a full refund or a credit toward a future course — whichever you'd prefer — regardless of how much notice you're able to give. Just let Jenna know when you're able to. This applies whatever the timeframes above say.

Missed sessions. Individual sessions aren't refundable. If you miss one, Jenna will do what she can to help you catch up before the next session, but a full make-up session can't always be arranged in a five-week course.

If Jenna has to cancel. If the course doesn't run, or a session can't go ahead and can't be rescheduled, you'll receive a full refund of the affected portion. If too few couples enrol, we'll tell you at least seven days before the start date and refund you in full.

Materials. The HypnoBirthing® textbook, Rainbow Relaxation and Affirmations downloads and parent handout spiral are ordered for you individually. Once ordered or issued, their cost isn't refundable and will be deducted from any refund.

Recording. Sessions are not recorded, by you or by us. This is a condition of the HypnoBirthing® Institute's teaching standards, not a Shibui Health preference. There are no recordings available if you miss a session.

Paying by instalments. If you pay using a buy-now-pay-later option, your payment agreement is with that provider, not with Shibui Health. Any refund is processed back through them and your remaining instalments are adjusted accordingly. You remain responsible for instalments until the refund is processed.

How to cancel. In writing to Jenna, by email or text to 0455 081 480. The date we receive your message is the date used above.
