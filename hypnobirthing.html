Birth doesn't have to be something that happens to you.

HypnoBirthing is a complete birth education program built around the idea that birth doesn't need to be frightening, over-managed or traumatic — that with the right preparation, you can feel calm, capable and genuinely involved, whatever your birth ends up looking like. The program teaches breathing, deep relaxation and visualisation techniques to work with your body through labour rather than against it.

What the classes cover

Practical techniques, not just theory — for you and your birth partner.

Breathing & relaxation Specific breathing and deep-relaxation techniques to reduce pain and fear during labour.
Visualisation Guided visualisation practice to work with your body's natural birthing ability, rather than tensing against it.
Your birth partner Classes are built for two — your partner learns the techniques alongside you and how to support you through labour.
Any birth plan The techniques are taught to support a calm, informed experience whether you're planning a vaginal birth, a caesarean, or a home birth.
Who teaches it Jenna Keygan, Endorsed Midwife and certified HypnoBirthing Practitioner — the same clinician who may also be providing your midwifery care.
