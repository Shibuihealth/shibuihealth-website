Privacy

How we handle your information.

A short, plain-language summary. Last updated 20 November 2026.

What we collect and why

If you send an enquiry we collect the details you enter (name, email, optional phone number and due date, and your message) so we can reply. If you become a patient we collect the health information needed to care for you and keep a clinical record. We don't use your details for unrelated marketing.

Who sees it

Our clinicians and practice staff, and the services we use to run the practice (our website host, our booking system Halaxy, our AI scribe Heidi, and email tools). Some may store information outside Australia. We share health information with others only with your consent or where the law allows or requires it.

AI scribe (Heidi)

Our clinicians may use Heidi, an AI scribe, to help write clinical notes. With your consent, it listens during your appointment and drafts a summary note. It is not a word-for-word record. Your clinician checks and corrects the note before it is saved to your health record, and Heidi does not make decisions about your care.

The audio is turned into text as we talk and is not kept. Draft notes are held in Heidi for a limited time and your record is kept in our clinical system. Heidi states that health information is not used to train AI.

We will ask you at the start of each appointment. You can say no, or change your mind at any time, and your care will be the same either way.

Access, correction and complaints

To see or correct the information we hold about you, or to raise a concern, email admin@shibuihealth.com.au and we'll respond promptly. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (privacy) or the Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency (AHPRA, practitioner conduct).
