Privacy

How we handle your information.

A short, plain-language summary. Last updated 21 November 2026.

What we collect and why

If you send an enquiry we collect the details you enter (name, email, optional phone number and due date, and your message) so we can reply. If you become a patient we collect the health information needed to care for you and keep a clinical record. We don't use your details for unrelated marketing.

Who sees it

Our clinicians and practice staff, and the services we use to run the practice (our website host, our booking system Halaxy, our payment provider Stripe, the platforms that host our member community and online sessions, and email tools). Some may store information outside Australia. We share health information with others only with your consent or where the law allows or requires it.

The Circle: membership and community

Payments. Membership is billed by Stripe, which collects your name, email and payment details directly. We never see or store your card details. Stripe is a third-party provider and may store information outside Australia. You can view your receipts, update your card or cancel from your Stripe billing area.

The members' group. Our private group runs on Facebook, owned by Meta, and we don't control it. To join you need a Facebook account, and Meta's own privacy terms apply to anything you do there, including storing information outside Australia. We see your join request and anything you post in the group. Other members can see what you post, so please don't share personal health or clinical details there.

Online sessions. Monthly education sessions run on Microsoft Teams and are recorded for members who can't attend. If you join a session your name may appear in the recording, and anything you say or type may be captured. If you'd rather not be recorded, you can keep your camera off and send questions to Jen privately instead.

To leave the group, remove a post, or ask us to delete what we hold about your membership, email us at the address below.

Access, correction and complaints

To see or correct the information we hold about you, or to raise a concern, email admin@shibuihealth.com.au and we'll respond promptly. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (privacy) or the Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency (AHPRA, practitioner conduct).
