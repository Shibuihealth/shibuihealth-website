Privacy & telehealth

How we handle your information.

A plain-language summary. Last updated 25 October 2026.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry we collect the details you enter: your name, email, optional phone number and due date, your message and your consent. When you book a phone consult through Halaxy, your booking and health information are handled in Halaxy, our practice management system. During consultations we collect the health information needed to care for you and keep a clinical record.

Why we collect it

To reply to your enquiry, arrange and provide your care, meet our professional and legal obligations, and manage our practice. We don't use enquiry details for unrelated marketing.

Who sees it

Our clinicians and practice staff, and the service providers we use to run the practice (for example our website host and Halaxy). Some providers may store information on servers outside Australia. We disclose health information to others only with your consent or where the law allows or requires it.

Please keep enquiries non-clinical

Website forms and email aren't the right place for clinical details. Please leave those for your consultation.

Phone (telehealth) consultations

Phone consults let us assess many problems without travel, but some conditions can't be safely assessed by phone. If that's the case we'll tell you and advise on next steps, which may include an in-person assessment or another service. Please take the call somewhere private with good reception. By booking a phone consult you agree to receive your care this way; you can ask us to arrange an alternative at any time where one is available. Emergencies aren't suitable for phone consults — call 000.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you by emailing admin@shibuihealth.com.au. If you have a concern or complaint, please tell us first and we'll respond promptly. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner about privacy concerns, or the Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency (AHPRA) or the Tasmanian Health Complaints Commissioner about a health practitioner's conduct.
