Privacy

How we handle your information.

A short, plain-language summary. Last updated 31 October 2026.

What we collect and why

If you send an enquiry we collect the details you enter (name, email, optional phone number and due date, and your message) so we can reply. If you become a patient we collect the health information needed to care for you and keep a clinical record. We don't use your details for unrelated marketing.

Who sees it

Our clinicians and practice staff, and the services we use to run the practice (our website host, our booking system Halaxy, our member community platform, and email tools). Some may store information outside Australia. We share health information with others only with your consent or where the law allows or requires it.

Our member community

If you join The Circle, our private online community, we collect the details you give when you sign up (such as your name and email) and anything you choose to post. The community runs on Circle (circle.so), a third-party platform that stores this information and may do so outside Australia. Other members can see what you post, so please don't share personal health or clinical details there. To have your member account or posts removed, email us at the address below.

Access, correction and complaints

To see or correct the information we hold about you, or to raise a concern, email admin@shibuihealth.com.au and we'll respond promptly. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (privacy) or the Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency (AHPRA, practitioner conduct).
