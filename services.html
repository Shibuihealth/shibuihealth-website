Care & pricing

Three ways we care for you, each led by the clinician whose name is on it.

Nurse practitioner phone consults can be booked online — no referral required. In-person and home visits, midwifery care and HypnoBirthing® classes are arranged by enquiry. Medicare rebates may apply to eligible consultations. Fees below are consultation fees only; any tests, treatments or products are costed separately.

Not for emergencies. If you or someone else has a life-threatening condition or is in danger, call 000 now. Shibui Health does not provide emergency care.

01

Repair

Minor injury & illness

For minor injuries and illnesses that need a clinician's assessment sooner rather than later: cuts, sprains, minor burns, infections, bites and everyday illnesses. Non-life-threatening presentations only.

What's included: a full history, assessment and advice or treatment as clinically appropriate, prescriptions where clinically indicated, and a written plan — including when to come back and what to watch for in the meantime.

How it works: Repair consults are currently offered by phone, which suits many minor problems. Some injuries need hands-on care. If that's the case we'll say so, and either arrange an in-person or home visit where we can (by enquiry) or point you to the right service.

02

Reshape

Weight management

Personalised weight management with a nurse practitioner — your history, your health picture and your goals set the plan, not a one-size template.

What's included: an initial consult covering your full medical and weight history, a plan built around what's realistic for your life, and scheduled review appointments so the plan adjusts as your results come in.

Well suited to: anyone who wants a clinically supervised, evidence-based approach to weight management rather than a generic program.

Book a Reshape phone consult
03

Reborn

Midwifery-led antenatal & postnatal care

Midwifery-led care with an endorsed midwife during pregnancy and in the weeks after your baby is born — one known midwife, rather than whoever's rostered on.

What's included: midwifery-led antenatal and postnatal consultations and services — antenatal visits built around you, preparation for birth and early parenting, and postnatal follow-up for you and your baby.

À la carte services: Reborn also includes à la carte midwifery services you can choose as you need them, arranged by enquiry. One of these is The Circle, a membership for $59 a month (cancel anytime) with monthly education sessions, an "Ask Jen" call and a local community. Circle members also get priority booking for antenatal and postnatal care packages.

Good to know: Reborn is arranged by enquiry and isn't bookable online. Jenna doesn't attend homebirths, and Reborn doesn't include attendance at your birth.

HypnoBirthing® classes are a separate childbirth education course, enrolled in and priced separately. You don't need to be a Reborn client to attend, and Reborn never requires the course. Read more about the classes →

Consultation fees

Set, published, and the same for everyone. No referral required.

All fees in AUD.
Consultation Provider Fee
Standard consult Nurse Practitioner $75.00AUD
Extended / initial consult Nurse Practitioner $120.00AUD
Standard consult Endorsed Midwife · by enquiry $125.00AUD

Fees are payable at the time of the consultation. If you're a new patient, your first phone consult is a private fee with no Medicare rebate. For existing patients, a Medicare rebate may apply to eligible consultations and is processed after your visit — eligibility depends on Medicare's rules. If cost is a barrier to care, mention it when you book or enquire; we'd rather talk it through than have you skip a visit. HypnoBirthing® course fees are separate and confirmed by Jenna — see the HypnoBirthing® page.
